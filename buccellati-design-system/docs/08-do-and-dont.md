# 08 · Do's & Don'ts

> If this page conflicts with anything else in the system, **this page wins**.

## Do

- **Default to Avorio** backgrounds. Reserve Blu Buccellati for **Cover**,
  **Section divider**, and **Closing** only.
- **Treat every slide as a page in an art-book** — wide margins, one idea.
- **Garamond for headings, Gill Sans for body, Georgia for data.** Three
  fonts. No more.
- **Source-cite every statistic** in 8pt Gill Sans Cenere at the lower-left.
- **Small caps with tracked spacing** (+200) for eyebrows and labels.
- **Use the honeycomb (tulle) motif as the Maison's signature mark** —
  once per section, maximum.
- **Build every layout from the Slide Master.** Never format slide-by-slide.
- **Embed fonts** before sharing externally (File → Options → Save → Embed
  fonts in the file).
- **Run the PowerPoint Accessibility Checker** before final export.
- **Lead chart slides with the answer.** A one-sentence takeaway in 14pt
  Gill Sans Italic immediately below the title.
- **Use Oro Antico for positive and Grafite for negative.** Never red /
  green.
- **Snap every element to the 12-column grid.** Nothing placed by eye.
- **Write declarative slide titles in sentence case** — *"Macri remains the
  foundation of the wholesale ladder"*, never *"Strategy: A Path Forward."*

## Don't

- ✗ **Don't use Calibri, Aptos, Arial,** or any default Microsoft font as a
  display face.
- ✗ **Don't use pure white** `#FFFFFF` (use Avorio `#F5F1EA`) or **pure black**
  `#000000` (use Nero Inchiostro `#1A1A1A`).
- ✗ **Don't use saturated digital colours** — green, red, orange, purple,
  teal — anywhere. Even in charts.
- ✗ **Don't use 3D charts**, drop shadows, glows, soft edges, rounded
  corners, or gradients.
- ✗ **Don't use clip art, emoji, AI-generated illustration, or stock luxury
  imagery.** (Champagne, yachts, sunglasses, business handshakes.)
- ✗ **Don't justify body text.** Justified copy creates rivers and reads
  corporate-default. Left, ragged right.
- ✗ **Don't repeat the Buccellati logo on every content slide** — the small
  "B" monogram in the footer is the content-slide mark.
- ✗ **Don't co-brand with Richemont** without checking Richemont brand
  guidelines.
- ✗ **Don't apply Microsoft 365 Designer "Design Ideas"** — it will violate
  every rule above.
- ✗ **Don't use the words "luxury," "premium," "exclusive,"** or "world-class"
  in headlines. The Maison's positioning is conveyed by the design system
  itself.
- ✗ **Don't use Title Case** for slide titles. Sentence case only.
- ✗ **Don't lead a chart with the data.** Lead with the takeaway.
- ✗ **Don't tilt images, round corners on portraits, or use circular
  crops.**
- ✗ **Don't use bullet points with round dots.** The system bullet is the
  em-dash in Oro Antico.
- ✗ **Don't use exclamation marks.** Anywhere.

## When tempted to be decorative

Ask: *would Mario Buccellati put this in a boutique window?*

The Maison's restraint is the design. Empty space is a Buccellati signal.
A confident sentence on a page of generous whitespace is more Buccellati
than three competing ones.

When in doubt, take something away.
