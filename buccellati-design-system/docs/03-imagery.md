# 03 · Imagery

> Photography is craft, not decoration. Every image on a Buccellati slide
> earns its place by showing the hand, the bench, the metal, or the city
> the Maison is from. Stock luxury imagery is the cardinal sin.

## Subject matter — in priority order

1. **Macro craft photography** — engraved gold, the burin in the artisan's
   hand, honeycomb piercing, hammer marks on silver, the workbench itself
2. **Product on a neutral ground** — a single jewel against linen, marble,
   or velvet
3. **Heritage imagery** — Mario Buccellati portrait, archival sketches,
   the 1919 Milan boutique, Place Vendôme, New York
4. **Italian context** — Renaissance architecture, Venetian lace, brocades,
   the Duomo, the Brera, courtyards in Milan
5. **Editorial portraits** — leadership, artisans, and clients photographed
   in available natural light, never on a white seamless

## Treatment rules

- **Aspect:** full-bleed 16:9 *or* a generous letterboxed image inside a
  0.6" frame of Avorio. No floating crops, no irregular shapes.
- **Colour:** prefer warm, slightly desaturated images. **Never** apply
  Instagram filters, drop shadows, vignettes, or rounded corners.
- **Lighting:** chiaroscuro — single warm light source, deep shadow,
  reminiscent of Italian Renaissance painting.
- **Focus:** shallow depth of field on craft macros; sharp on portraits.
- **Orientation:** images snap to the 12-column grid; **never** tilt.

## Image placement

- **One image per content slide, maximum.** A second image is a second slide.
- Image rights / credit in 8 pt Gill Sans MT at the lower-right corner, in
  Cenere `#8A8A86` — barely there but present.
- Captions, where used, sit in a 4"-wide block over a 60% opacity Avorio
  scrim (see layout **07 Image-led full-bleed**).
- Portraits in **13 Team / Org** sit on a Pergamena mat with consistent
  lighting, angle, and crop ratio across every member. No drop shadows.
  No circular crops. No coloured borders.

## What never appears

- Generic "luxury" stock: champagne, yachts, sunglasses, business handshakes
- AI-generated jewellery
- Clipart, emoji, illustrated icons of jewels
- Magazine-style photo collages
- Images of competitor brands or products

## Alt text — required on every image

Every `<picture>`, `<img>`, embedded photograph, chart, and logo carries
meaningful alt text. The AI prompt generates one automatically for each
`[IMAGE: …]` placeholder. A good alt text describes the *subject and what
it conveys*, not the file name:

- **Good:** *"Macro photograph of yellow-gold rigato engraving, raking light
  from the left, showing parallel hairlines."*
- **Bad:** *"image1.jpg"* or *"jewellery"*

## Sourcing & rights

- Original Buccellati photography — preferred
- Richemont group archive — second
- Licensed editorial (e.g. Skira monograph plates) — third, with credit
- Stock — only with explicit creative-director sign-off

## Production specifications

| Use case             | Resolution                          | Format                |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Projector deck       | 1920 × 1080 px at 144 DPI            | JPEG quality 85       |
| Print PDF            | 300 DPI at final placed size         | TIFF or 100% JPEG     |
| Web preview          | Compressed to ≤ 200 KB per image     | WebP / JPEG progressive |

Embedded images in `.pptx` should be re-sampled to placed size before
embedding — PowerPoint does not down-sample on save and 30 MB decks
become 300 MB decks in a hurry.
