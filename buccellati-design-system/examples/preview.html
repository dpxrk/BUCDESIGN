Design system · v1.0.0

Buccellati Executive Presentation

A reusable design system for the Milanese high-jewellery Maison — engineered so a CEO-level deliverable can be produced by Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini and stay unmistakably Buccellati.

01 · Colour

The palette

A monochrome ivory-and-black backbone with one regal blue and a warm matte gold. Saturated digital colour is the violation.

Avorio#F5F1EA Default slide background — never pure white
Nero Inchiostro#1A1A1A Primary text — never pure black
Pergamena#EAE2D2 Section divider alternate
Blu Buccellati#1B2A4E Cover · section · closing only
Oro Antico#A8894E Hairlines, eyebrows, positive deltas
Oro Chiaro#C9A961 Highlight states, chart secondary
Grafite#4A4A48 Secondary text · negative deltas
Cenere#8A8A86 Tertiary text · axis labels · footers
